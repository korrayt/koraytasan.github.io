CINEMATIC / NARRATIVE

A soundtrack for the stories we carry.

SUSSUZ SOUNDTRACKS grows out of the SUSSUZ story universe, inspired by the landscape and changing lights of Ankara’s western edge. Not simply a background score, the music is imagined as another narrator — allowing a place, a memory or an unfinished conversation to be heard before anyone speaks.

Distant textures, intimate melodies, restrained electronic production and cinematic dynamics come together to leave room for the story. Songs and cues can evolve as the wider film and interactive world takes shape.
