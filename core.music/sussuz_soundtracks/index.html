FILM SOUNDTRACK • COMPOSED BY KHRYSAOR

A soundtrack for the stories we carry.

SUSSUZ SOUNDTRACK grows out of the SUSSUZ film and narrative universe, inspired by the nocturnal landscape and changing lights of Ankara’s western edge. Not simply a background score, the music is imagined as an authoritative narrator — allowing a place, a memory or an unfinished conversation to be heard before anyone speaks.

Composed, arranged and engineered by khrysaor, the album combines analog warmth, cinematic orchestral sub-bass, and authentic street acoustics.
