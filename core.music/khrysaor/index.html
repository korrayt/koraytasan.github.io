NOCTURNAL / ATMOSPHERIC

From the hills of Cihangir.

khrysaor draws its visual identity from Cihangir, Istanbul: layered apartments, alleys falling toward the sea, late lights in old windows and the uneasy stillness between a city’s rush and its early morning quiet.

Rather than prescribing a single genre, this space is built around atmosphere. Urban sounds, melodic fragments and emotional detail form a nocturnal palette. Future music and visuals can be added here as the project takes shape.

An evolving artist identity
