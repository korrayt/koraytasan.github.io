ABOUT THE LABEL

Independent sound.
Distinctive worlds.

CORE MUSIC brings together multiple musical identities, each with its own voice, geography and imagination. The work moves between cinematic storytelling, nocturnal electronic music and culture-rooted celebration.

01

Story first.

We approach music as a creative world: a mood, a character, a place and the feeling a listener takes with them.

02

Different by design.

Not every project should sound the same. Every identity deserves its own sound and visual language.

03

Always creating.

Music releases, lyrics, soundtrack ideas and new production methods all have a place in the CORE MUSIC universe.
