ABOUT THE LABEL

Independent sound.
Distinctive worlds.

CORE MUSIC LONDON brings together multiple musical identities, each with its own voice, geography and imagination. The roster moves between cinematic storytelling (SUSSUZ SOUNDTRACK), nocturnal electronic music (CORE), Cihangir street poetry (khrysaor), and culture-rooted celebration (ANGARALI KEREMSİ).

01

Story first.

We approach music as a creative world: a mood, a character, a place and the feeling a listener takes with them.

02

Different by design.

Not every project should sound the same. Every artist identity deserves its own acoustic frequency and visual language.

03

Always creating.

Studio albums, lyric archives, film scoring and next-generation audio engineering all have a home in CORE MUSIC LONDON.
