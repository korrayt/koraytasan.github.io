ANKARA / CELEBRATION

A little mischief. A lot of rhythm.

ANGARALI KEREMSİ brings sharp observation and a playful point of view to dance-ready Ankara-inspired music. Every title offers a little wink; the rhythms are made to travel from a neighbourhood celebration to a packed wedding floor.

The visual world stays warm and recognisable: lights coming on as the party gets going, familiar streets, shared tables and an unmistakably local sense of humour. The project sits under CORE MUSIC while keeping its own personality.
