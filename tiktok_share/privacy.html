Privacy Policy — team.core

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how team.core (https://koraytasan.com/tiktok_share/) collects, uses, and protects information when you connect your TikTok account.

1. Data Accessed via TikTok APIs

  • user.info.basic: Used solely to display your TikTok profile nickname, avatar, and creator posting parameters within the studio interface.
  • video.upload & video.publish: Used solely to upload and publish video files that you explicitly select and submit through the team.core studio.

2. Data Storage & Security

OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens are stored securely and used exclusively to perform user-initiated API requests. We never sell, rent, or share your personal data or TikTok credentials with third parties.

3. Revoking Access & Data Deletion

You may revoke team.core's access at any time directly from your TikTok app settings (Settings and Privacy → Security and Permissions → Apps and Services) or by contacting ben@koraytasan.com.
