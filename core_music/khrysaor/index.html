ARTIST • KHRYSAOR

From the hills of Cihangir.

khrysaor draws its visual and sonic identity from Cihangir, Istanbul: layered apartments, alleys falling toward the sea, late lights in old windows and the uneasy stillness between a city’s rush and its early morning quiet.

Rather than prescribing a single genre, khrysaor operates across narrative depth: urban poetry, organic melancholia, ambient field textures, and heavy modern rhythm. The catalogue is anchored by two official albums: the personal studio album "Low Battery" and the cinematic film score "SUSSUZ Soundtrack".
