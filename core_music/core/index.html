01 / THE STORY · ARTIST CORE

Some things are
better heard.

“You don't need my name.
You already know the feeling.”

There are things CORE wants to say. An explanation of who CORE is isn't one of them.

CORE makes music for what is difficult to say out loud. Moving between alternative R&B, dark pop and electronic textures, the songs deal in desire, distance, memory and the things we carry long after a moment has passed. CORE lets the music speak before the biography.

Operating within the creative world of Core Music London, CORE draws on the atmosphere of cities after dark: headlights in the rain, a room after someone leaves, the space between holding on and letting go. The sound moves between alternative R&B, dark pop, electronic production and trap-soul touches. The voice can be intimate, controlled, confrontational and unexpectedly vulnerable.

The songs are personal without offering a literal map back to the person behind them. They circle desire, loyalty, regret, freedom and the cost of being understood. The mystery isn't a riddle to solve. It is room for the listener's own story.

IN CORE'S OWN WORDS

“I have something to say. My name isn't it.”

Not a puzzle. Not a biography. Only what remains when the music stops.
