CORE is the flagship English-language artist of CORE MUSIC LONDON. From fast-moving nocturnal asphalt to a vulnerable late-night confession, each release opens a distinct emotional corner of the same cinematic audio universe.

The debut album "Kiss Me Wrong" brings together the acclaimed 7-song Lunar Phase Series. Official distribution has been submitted today to all global streaming platforms including Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal, Amazon Music and YouTube Music.
