ARTIST • ANGARALI KEREMSİ

A little mischief. A lot of rhythm.

ANGARALI KEREMSİ brings sharp observation, high-tempo celebration, and a playful point of view to Ankara-inspired dance and street music. Every title offers a knowing wink; the grooves are built to travel from a neighbourhood table to a packed wedding floor.

The visual world stays warm, humorous and unmistakably local: flashing neon, familiar streets, shared laughs and a proud local identity under the CORE MUSIC LONDON roster.
